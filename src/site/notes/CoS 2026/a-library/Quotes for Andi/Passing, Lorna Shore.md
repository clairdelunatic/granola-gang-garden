Lord Kelemvor, 
I beseech you to put this soul to rest
Azrael, guide him beyond the veil 
Free him from this mortal shell 
As he enters into the darkest night 
Through the sands of time, 
Until he is returned to the womb of un-life, 
Never to return as un-dead.
