We completed several tasks:
- Bram earned 200 gold by [[CoS 2026/Sessions/Quests/Completed/Win at bull-jumping\|bull-jumping]] at [[CoS 2026/Codex/NPCs (Friends)/Anikka and Cedric\|Anikka and Cedric]]'s carnival stall, collecting another 101 gold from the crowd.
- We earned 60 gold 20 silver after we [[CoS 2026/Sessions/Quests/Completed/Tame Rictavio's Sabre Tooth Tiger\|tamed Rictavio's cat]]

However, a fire broke out at the carnival, and panic ensued (at the disco). 
- During the confusion, Zar freed [[CoS 2026/Codex/Followers/Pelor and Midnight\|two seals]], for which, it turned out, [[CoS 2026/Codex/NPCs (Friends)/Madam Eva\|Madam Eva]] just happened to have a tank prepared in her caravan. 
- Despite the confusion, we noticed several figures (one of whom was probably [[CoS 2026/Codex/NPCs (Friends)/Yarak and Dabra\|Dabra]]) being carted off
