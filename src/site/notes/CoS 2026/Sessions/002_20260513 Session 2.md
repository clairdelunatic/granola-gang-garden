## Log

### Scene 1: Caravan Ride

We rode in Madam Eva's [[CoS 2026/Codex/Other/Patthana#Vardo\|vardo]] to Barovia. It was a five-hour trip.
- Through the windows, in the cold, impenetrable mist, we saw frightening (screaming) faces, which we later learned were the faces of souls caught in a kind of purgatory, frozen in agony. These souls are trapped in the [[CoS 2026/Codex/Locations/Barovia (Land)\|land of Barovia]], getting recycled endlessly. We were later advised by [[CoS 2026/Codex/NPCs (Friends)/Stanimir\|Stanimir]] not to stare at these faces; we would risk having nightmares.
	- Should Andi be able to do something about the trapped souls? Help them move along? #todo 
- The [[CoS 2026/Codex/Other/Patthana#Vardo\|vardo]] came to an abrupt halt as we heard [[CoS 2026/Codex/NPCs (Friends)/Stanimir\|Old Stanimir]] shouting, "Kyrie Yaga" (?) #todo
