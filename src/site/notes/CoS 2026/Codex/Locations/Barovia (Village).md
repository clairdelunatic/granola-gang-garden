### Bildrath's Mercantile (E1)
The largest supply store run by [[Bildrath Cantemir\|Bildrath Cantemir]]. You can get products from the Outside brought by [[CoS 2026/Codex/Factions/Vistani\|Vistani]] caravans.
### The Blood of the Vine (E2)
The largest tavern and inn, where people from out of town are directed to seek shelter and entertainment. [[CoS 2026/Codex/NPCs (Friends)/Stanimir\|Stanimir]] says its owned by [[CoS 2026/Codex/Factions/Vistani\|Vistani]] but operated by *[[CoS 2026/Codex/Other/Patthana#Balok\|balok]]s* (locals).
### Burgomaester's Mansion (E4)
The civic center of town and seat of its municipal government, as well as official residence of the "mayor." 
### Church of St. Bogdan (E5)
A temple to the [[CoS 2026/Codex/Factions/Morninglord\|Morninglord]]. It's hallowed ground so it is somewhat immune to the corrupting effect of Strahd's curse. It also houses the cemetery (E6). 
### Alastroi House (E7)
This is the address that [[CoS 2026/Codex/NPCs (Friends)/Sister Olivenka\|Sister Olivenka]] gave you for her old family house in the east side of town. Her full, secular name is Olivenka Alastroi. The house should be empty.
