# [[CoS 2026/Codex/Other/Patthana\|Patthana]] or the [[CoS 2026/Codex/Other/Patthana\|Vistani Language]]

Patthana, or “the secret, the tongue,” is the language of the [[CoS 2026/Codex/Factions/Vistani\|Vistani]].

### Balok
A local.
### Skurra
An outcast, someone who has been exiled from his native tashq an is considered a pariah. Also, camp followers who are not [[CoS 2026/Codex/Factions/Vistani\|Vistani]] but attach themselves to the caravan. They perform menial tasks and entertainment in exchange for being accepted back into a *[[CoS 2026/Codex/Other/Patthana#tashq\|#tashq]]*. In [[CoS 2026/Sessions/002_20260513 Session 2\|Session 2]], [[CoS 2026/Codex/NPCs (Friends)/Stanimir\|Stanimir]] claimed that Madam Eva's family were considered *skurra*.
### Tashq
plural *Tashuq*
A tribe or clan of [[CoS 2026/Codex/Factions/Vistani\|Vistani]] related by common rite and myth. Each of them has a specific occupation or specialty for which they are known. A caravan or encampment will include auxiliaries and craftsmen from different *tashuq*.
### Vardo
A house-wagon. A true mobile residence with lots of storage space for merchandise and every necessity of life that can be deployed to camp.
### Xorxa
An outsider, a stranger, a non-[[CoS 2026/Codex/Factions/Vistani\|Vistani]].
