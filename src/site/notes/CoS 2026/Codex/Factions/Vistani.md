This group is a mysterious group of merchants and performers led by the son of [[CoS 2026/Codex/NPCs (Friends)/Madam Eva\|Madam Eva]].

They travel to and from Barovia, but their method is mist-erious. Their caravans seem to apparate from this mist itself.


In [[CoS 2026/Sessions/002_20260513 Session 2\|Session 2]], we learned
- They have their own language.
- They are composed of six tribes.
- They rarely take people to the [[CoS 2026/Codex/Locations/Barovia (Land)\|land of Barovia]] with consent; usually, people are kidnapped.
- They are the "children of [[CoS 2026/Codex/NPCs (Friends)/Strahd von Zarovich\|Strahd]]," favored by him but requiring of them many sacrifices.
