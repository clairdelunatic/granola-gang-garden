# [[CoS 2026/Codex/Factions/Zealots\|Zealots]]

The **Zealots** of [[CoS 2026/Codex/Locations/Elturel, the Holy City\|Elturel]] are a faction of fanatics, forged in the campaigns to clear the Plaguelands and frontiers from monsters and undead.

Led by: **Thavius Kreeg**, the High Priest of Helm and a favorite to succeed Dhelt

Supported by: 
- Rich and devout nobles who yearn for more free reign in their business and to place their scions in the ranks of an expanded Church.
- War veterans
- Non-human or half-human pilgrims come from abroad.
## Pros
- a high degree of civil liberties for commoners
- political independence to the cities
- trade monopolies to noble houses
- 
## Cons 
- bureaucracy: a lot of complicated pacts, charters and treaty rights  . 
- 
## Beliefs/Goals
- Only gods that are both lawful and good should be allowed in the Church
- Other cults should be heavily restricted
- The priesthood should take a more active role in civilian administration 
 - Economic activity should be less under government control
 - Only the god Amauntor exists and all others are either his avatars or his enemies
