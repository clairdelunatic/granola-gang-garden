# [[CoS 2026/Codex/Factions/Legalists\|Legalists]]

The **Legalists** of [[CoS 2026/Codex/Locations/Elturel, the Holy City\|Elturel]] are supporters of the status quo. 

Led by: the aged **Ithamar Dhelt**, the current High Observer, and  mostly old humans but they have support of the non-religious factions and non-official cults.
## Pros
- a high degree of civil liberties for commoners
- political independence to the cities
- trade monopolies to noble houses
- 
## Cons 
- bureaucracy: a lot of complicated pacts, charters and treaty rights  . 
- 
## Beliefs/Goals
- Prioritize keeping the various factions intriguing with each other rather than discontent with the regime
- Believe all good _or_ lawful gods are equal and allowed into the official sponsorship of the Church and they tolerate neutral gods. Only Evil is beyond redemption.
