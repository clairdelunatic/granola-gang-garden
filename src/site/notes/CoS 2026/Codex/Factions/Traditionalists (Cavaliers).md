The **Traditionalists** of [[CoS 2026/Codex/Locations/Elturel, the Holy City\|Elturel]] are a recent but highly popular faction. They're settlers and penitents, people who see the [[CoS 2026/Codex/Factions/Hellriders\|Hellriders]]' value in their pacification campaigns more than in their religious vision.
Led by: the war hero **Tamal Thent**

Supported by: 
- Rich and devout nobles who yearn for more free reign in their business and to place their scions in the ranks of an expanded Church.
- War veterans
- Non-human or half-human pilgrims come from abroad.
## Beliefs/Goals
- They believe in order and civilization 
- The highest manifestation of Evil is the untamed wilderness
- Nature-aligned cults should be prohibited
- Restricting the rights of "exotic" races
- A more militarized society
