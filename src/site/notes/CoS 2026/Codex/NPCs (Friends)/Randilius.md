Randilius was a bookseller at the [[CoS 2026/Sessions/Events/Carnival of the Mists\|Carnival of the Mists]].

In [[CoS 2026/Sessions/001_20260429 Session 1\|Session 1]], he recognized and interacted with [[CoS 2026/PCs/Bram the Quiet\|Bram]] as if they were old acquaintances. He had an ancient tome, the incomplete [[CoS 2026/Codex/Other/Tome of Strahd\|Tome of Strahd]], which he delayed selling to [[CoS 2026/Codex/NPCs (Friends)/Stanimir\|Stanimir]] to drive up its price.
