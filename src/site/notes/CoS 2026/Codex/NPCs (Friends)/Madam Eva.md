# [[CoS 2026/Codex/NPCs (Friends)/Madam Eva\|Madam Eva]]

Madam Eva is a tarokka card reader and is the matriarch of the [[CoS 2026/Codex/Factions/Vistani\|Vistani]] merchants.

She is at least 700 years old. 

In [[CoS 2026/Sessions/002_20260513 Session 2\|Session 2]], [[CoS 2026/Codex/NPCs (Friends)/Stanimir\|Stanimir]] suggested she is the *babushka* of all the [[CoS 2026/Codex/Factions/Vistani\|Vistani]], that [everyone seems to be related to her](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RrmWFjnAP2E) in some way. [[CoS 2026/Codex/NPCs (Friends)/Stanimir\|Stanimir]] does not appear to be her biological son.
