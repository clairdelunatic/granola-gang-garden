# [[CoS 2026/Codex/NPCs (Friends)/Anikka and Cedric\|Anikka and Cedric]]
These conjoined goblin twins were the carnival hands who ran a bull-jumping stall.

In session 1, they rewarded the party with 200 gold for [[CoS 2026/Sessions/Quests/Completed/Win at bull-jumping\|putting on a good show]].
