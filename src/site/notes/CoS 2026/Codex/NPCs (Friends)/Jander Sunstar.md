# [[CoS 2026/Codex/NPCs (Friends)/Jander Sunstar\|Jander Sunstar]]

One of the few remaining survivors of the original crusade to Hell, he seems to send [[CoS 2026/PCs/Zar\|Zar]] on private missions. Prior to Session 1, he had sent [[CoS 2026/PCs/Zar\|Zar]] to [[CoS 2026/Codex/Locations/Barovia (Land)\|The Land of Barovia]]. 

In [[CoS 2026/Sessions/001_20260429 Session 1\|Session 1]], 
- he was described as having supernatural charm, ethereal beauty, and unsettling allure. 
- [[CoS 2026/PCs/Zar\|Zar]] had to pass a charisma save just to entering [[CoS 2026/Codex/NPCs (Friends)/Jander Sunstar\|Sunstar]]'s tent.
