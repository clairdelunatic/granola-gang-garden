## Backstory 

[[CoS 2026/PCs/Bram the Quiet\|Bram]] is a cloud giant monk of the Way of Mercy.

Standing just over eight feet tall, his presence is imposing, yet his demeanor is calm and controlled. His head is shaved, his frame powerful but composed, and his movements are deliberate rather than aggressive. A scar runs over his left eye, marking a past he does not speak of, and his eyes are mismatched. One blue, one green giving him an intense, unforgettable gaze. 

His hands are wrapped in cloth. One side clean, used for healing, and the other bound in red, reserved for combat. 

Heavy prayer beads rest around his neck, and his simple, worn robes reflect a life of discipline, travel, and hard labor. Though he appears intimidating at first glance, Bram carries himself with restraint and quiet purpose. 

He values balance believing that strength should be used to end suffering, not create it. He offers aid where he can, but does not hesitate to act when necessary. Bram travels not for glory, but with a personal goal. to find the truth of where he came from and the family he never knew.
